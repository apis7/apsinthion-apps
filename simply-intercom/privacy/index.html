Simply Intercom

Privacy Policy · Published by Apsinthion · Effective October 15, 2026

Short version

Simply Intercom collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It has no internet access, no account, no ads and no analytics. Your voice goes from the microphone straight to your Bluetooth speaker and is never recorded or saved.

What the app stores

Only its settings, kept on your phone: tone frequency, volume-key push to talk, and keep screen on. They never leave the device.

What is collected

Nothing. The app has no network permission and includes no third-party SDKs that collect data. We receive no information about you or how you use the app.

Permissions and why

  • Microphone: to pick up your voice while you hold Push to Talk. The audio is played live through the speaker and kept only in memory for a fraction of a second while it is played. It is never written to storage or sent anywhere.
  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): to check whether a Bluetooth speaker is connected.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show an ongoing notification while the intercom is on, so audio keeps playing if you switch apps.

Device backups

The app opts out of Android backup, so its settings are not copied to Google or any cloud.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Simply Intercom. That removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
