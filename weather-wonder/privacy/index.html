Weather Wonder

Privacy Policy · Published by Apsinthion · Effective October 23, 2026

Short version

Apsinthion collects nothing. Weather Wonder has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it never reads your GPS location. To show your weather, the app sends the zip codes you enter, and their map coordinates, directly from your phone to public weather services. We don't run a server and never receive that information.

What leaves your phone, and where it goes

All requests go straight from your phone to these services over encrypted HTTPS connections:

  • Zippopotam.us receives the zip code you type and returns its city and coordinates (latitude and longitude). zippopotam.us
  • Open-Meteo (forecast and air quality) receives those coordinates and returns the weather and air quality. Open-Meteo privacy policy
  • US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) receives those coordinates and returns any active weather alerts. NWS privacy policy

Like any website, these services can see your phone's IP address when the app connects to them. Their handling of requests is covered by their own policies, linked above. The app sends nothing else: no name, no device identifiers, no contacts, no GPS position.

If you tap Radar or Forecast, the app opens a weather map or the National Weather Service forecast page for that location in your web browser. Those websites are covered by their own privacy policies.

What the app stores

Only on your phone: your zip codes, your settings (units, refresh interval, radar choice) and the most recent weather it downloaded, so the widget can show it instantly.

What Apsinthion collects

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we receive no information about you or how you use the app.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download the weather.
  • Run at startup and keep awake briefly: so the widget keeps refreshing on its schedule after a reboot.

The app does not request location permission.

Device backups

If you use Android's own backup, the app's settings and zip codes may be included in your Google backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; Apsinthion has no access to it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Weather Wonder. That removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and Apsinthion collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
