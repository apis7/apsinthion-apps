Nertz Scorekeeper

Privacy Policy · Published by Apsinthion · Effective September 30, 2026

The short version: nothing leaves your phone. Nertz Scorekeeper has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet permission. It cannot send your data anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

  • Player names you enter, and the color assigned to each.
  • Games — round scores, who called Nertz, winners, and how long games took.
  • Your settings — winning score, theme, sounds and haptics.

All of it is kept only in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. We (Apsinthion) receive no personal or usage data of any kind. There are no third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporters or ads in the app.

Permissions

  • Vibrate — for optional haptic feedback.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or network access.

Device backups

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include this app's data in your personal Google backup, as it does for most apps. That backup is controlled by you and Google; we never have access to it.

Deleting your data

Remove players on the Players screen, clear game history in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
